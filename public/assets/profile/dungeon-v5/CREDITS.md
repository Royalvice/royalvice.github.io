# Cabin assets

## Movement
Five-character sprites final v3.9, supplied by the site owner from `five-character-sprites-final-v3.9`. 45 clips, 760 frames. Original JSON frame rate, crop, duration, and foot pivots are preserved; PNG sheets are losslessly encoded as WebP. Rightward movement mirrors leftward clips.

## New animation and props
25 character action sheets and seven Ruru sheets generated with OpenAI ImageGen from the user-approved character references, September 2026. Generation prompts, original sheets, refinement originals, and eight-frame review contact sheets are retained in `artifacts/dungeon-rebuild/`. Tanjiro and Nezuko shelf collectibles are generated from their character descriptions. Runtime sprites are chroma-keyed and foot-aligned; repeating gestures use return frames, and sleep ends on a held sleeping pose.

Ruru's only likeness reference is the user-supplied orange mascot with an orange top ornament, blue eyes, one front tooth, and orange shorts. No generic capybara asset was substituted.

Existing Spirited Away / One Piece posters, furniture atlas, TV game, Returning Home audio, and shared seascape retain their existing provenance.

## 3D collection
See `models/sources.json` for individual original project links, uploader, source archive SHA-256, download date, and conversion notes. Jack, Ace, Taro, and Ultraman 80 are actual downloaded Source models. Their original texture maps are preserved; the existing skeleton was posed for display and converted to GLB. No replacement character geometry was generated.

The four SFMLab downloads are labelled CC0 by their uploader, TengenGenesic. The website states that the uploader-selected license is not verified by moderators; character rights are not transferred by that label. The archive material namespace identifies Ultimo.

This collection is incomplete: original Ultraman, Zoffy, Ultraseven, and Leo have no deployable asset in this directory. No substitute is shown in their reserved positions.
