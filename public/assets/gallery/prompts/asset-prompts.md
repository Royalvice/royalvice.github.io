## Cabinet Replacement Asset Requirements

- File format: `.glb` preferred.
- Texture maps: albedo/base color, roughness, metallic, normal, optional emissive/transmission.
- Pivot: centered on base contact point.
- Scale: trophy top should fit within a 1m virtual cube.
- Preview: transparent PNG/WebP for documentation.
